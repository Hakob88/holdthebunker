Privacy Policy

Hold the Bunker · Effective November 25, 2026

In short

Hold the Bunker does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data on your device

Your progress (stars, unlocked soldiers and maps, co-op records) and your settings (difficulty, sound, haptics) are saved only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, the game sends your achievements and leaderboard scores to Apple's Game Center so they appear in your profile and on leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we don't receive your Game Center information. You can play without Game Center.

Local network (multiplayer)

For co-op and versus, the game asks for Local Network access to find and connect to a nearby friend's device. The two devices exchange only game moves and the soldiers chosen, directly with each other; nothing goes over the internet or to us. Hosted games appear to nearby devices under a random name such as “Bunker 1234”. You can turn this access off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network; single-player games don't need it.

Children

Because no personal data is collected from anyone, none is collected from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
